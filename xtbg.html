XTBG 西双版纳热带植物园

Xishuangbanna Tropical Botanical Garden

Yunnan, China

In August of 2026, I spent a month designing and building with the Chinese Academy of Science at Xishuangbanna Tropical Botanical Garden.

The geographic positioning of the garden, nestled between rivers and mountains downwind of the Tibetan plateau, contributes to a uniquely tropical climate for such a northern latitude.

奇花异卉园

The Distinctive Plant Garden

My primary project was to redesign one of the institution’s most popular gardens, the “Distinctive Plant Garden.”

Working with the gardeners each morning gave me a keen eye into how people moved through the garden. I quickly noticed commotion around a very special plant.

A focal point of the garden is the unique Dancing Grass Plant Codariocalyx motorius. Tour guides sing to it and play music to get the leaves to move. The native Dai people in the area have a legend about a girl who was an amazing dancer and after tragically drowning in the surrounding river, her passion lives on in these plants that became bountiful after she passed.
